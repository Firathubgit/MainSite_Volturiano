Before you run these prompts, you need to make the biggest architectural decision
for your platform. Because Vercel kills Serverless Functions after 10-60 seconds, 
it cannot host your Express backend (`server/index.js`), which runs long sandboxes.

Here are your options for hosting the backend. Read them, make a choice, and then
use the Megaprompts below.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DECISION MATRIX: BACKEND HOSTING OPTIONS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🟢 OPTION A: Railway.app (Highly Recommended)
- Pros: Incredible developer experience. You just link your GitHub, point it to
  the `web/server` folder, and it automatically builds and runs it. Great pricing 
  ($5/mo hobby tier gives you plenty of compute). Easy env var management.
- Cons: Slightly more expensive at scale compared to a raw VPS.

🟡 OPTION B: Render.com (Solid Alternative)
- Pros: Similar to Railway, very easy Git-based deployment. Offers a free tier 
  (though it spins down when inactive, which you don't want for production).
- Cons: Builds can be a bit slower than Railway. 

🟠 OPTION C: DigitalOcean Droplet / AWS EC2 / Custom VPS
- Pros: Cheapest option at high scale. Complete control over the OS.
- Cons: You have to configure everything manually (Node.js, PM2, Nginx, SSL certs,
  GitHub Actions for CI/CD). It's a huge DevOps headache.

Recommendation: Go with Railway.app. It pairs perfectly with Vercel frontend.
